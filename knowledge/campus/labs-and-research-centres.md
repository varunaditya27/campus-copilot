# Labs and Research Centres

## Centre for AI and Data Systems

Affiliated primarily with Information Science and Engineering and Computer Science and Engineering, and housed on the third floor of the Innovation and Computing Centre. The centre's faculty lead is Dr. Farhan Sheikh, with Dr. Ritika Chawla serving as associate lead. Ongoing focus areas include applied machine learning, natural language processing, recommendation systems, and large-scale data infrastructure. The centre currently supports six active postgraduate research projects and a rotating set of undergraduate mentorship slots opened each semester.

Students interested in joining typically begin by attending the centre's open lab-tour session held in the first two weeks of each semester, after which interested students submit a short statement of interest to the centre coordinator. Most undergraduates start with a scoped task under a postgraduate researcher before taking on independent work in their final year.

## Embedded Systems and IoT Lab

Affiliated primarily with Electronics and Communication Engineering, located on the ground floor of the Innovation and Computing Centre. The lab's faculty lead is Dr. Shalini D'Souza. Ongoing focus areas include microcontroller-based system design, wireless sensor networks, embedded machine learning on constrained hardware, and Internet-of-Things prototyping for campus and agricultural applications. The lab frequently supplies component kits and bench access for final-year projects originating in the department's embedded systems elective.

Students can request lab access through the department office by submitting a project proposal co-signed by a supervising faculty member; access is granted for a semester at a time and renewed on request.

## Cybersecurity Research Lab

Affiliated primarily with Computer Science and Engineering and Information Science and Engineering, located in the Cybersecurity Lab space on the second floor of the Innovation and Computing Centre. The lab's faculty lead is Mr. Karan Malhotra. Ongoing focus areas include applied network security, secure software development practices, and authorized vulnerability research conducted against isolated lab infrastructure rather than live systems.

All security research and testing conducted through the lab follows a written authorization process: a student must have a signed scope-of-work document from the supervising faculty member before running any scanning, exploitation, or penetration-testing activity, even against university-owned systems. Unauthorized testing against production university systems is treated as a serious disciplinary matter regardless of intent. Students interested in joining should first complete the department's introductory security elective or demonstrate equivalent coursework.

## Robotics and Automation Lab

Affiliated primarily with Mechanical Engineering and Electrical and Electronics Engineering, housed in a dedicated workshop bay adjacent to the Mechanical Engineering workshop floor. The lab's faculty lead is Dr. Kiran Kumar, with Ms. Neha Kapoor serving as associate lead for the automation track. Ongoing focus areas include robotic manipulation, autonomous navigation, industrial automation, and control-systems research, frequently combined with embedded and machine-learning components contributed by students from other departments.

The lab hosts an annual robotics showcase each spring where ongoing student projects are demonstrated to faculty and visiting industry judges. Students join primarily through the Robotics and Embedded Systems Community or by direct application to the lab coordinator with a short project proposal.

## Power and Renewable Energy Lab

Affiliated primarily with Electrical and Electronics Engineering, located on the second floor of the Main Academic Block adjacent to the department's power systems laboratory. The lab's faculty lead is Dr. Rekha Kulkarni. Ongoing focus areas include power-systems analysis, renewable-energy integration, smart-grid research, and energy storage systems. The lab maintains a small rooftop solar test installation used for live data collection in several undergraduate projects.

Interested students should approach the lab coordinator during the third year, since most project slots are allocated to students who have completed the department's power systems and control systems core courses.

## Materials and Manufacturing Lab

Affiliated primarily with Mechanical Engineering, located within the department's workshop complex. The lab's faculty lead is Dr. Deepak Bhatia, with Dr. Anjali Verma leading the materials characterization track. Ongoing focus areas include materials characterization, additive manufacturing, and computational manufacturing design, supported by the department's 3D printing and CNC machining facilities.

Students typically gain access to the lab through the second-year manufacturing practice course, after which interested students can apply for continued project access through the lab coordinator.

## Getting Involved in Research

Students interested in research at any of these centres typically follow a similar path: identify a centre whose focus area matches their interest, reach out to the faculty lead or associate lead by email or during posted office hours, and discuss expectations, time commitment, and whether the involvement is for academic credit, a paid research assistantship, or voluntary experience. Most centres expect students to complete a smaller, well-scoped task before taking on independent research responsibility.

Availability of research assistantships and specific ongoing projects changes each semester; students should confirm current openings directly with the centre rather than assuming continuity from a previous term.

## Interdisciplinary Use

Several centres deliberately overlap in focus. Embedded artificial intelligence work, for example, spans both the Centre for AI and Data Systems and the Embedded Systems and IoT Lab, and several robotics projects draw jointly on the Robotics and Automation Lab and the Centre for AI and Data Systems for perception and control components. Students pursuing interdisciplinary final-year projects are encouraged to reach out to faculty across more than one centre and to secure co-guidance early, since project proposals spanning two centres typically require sign-off from both faculty leads.
