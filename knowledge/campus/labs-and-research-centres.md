# Labs and Research Centres

> **Demo corpus document:** Centre names, focus areas, and affiliations below are fictionalized workshop data.

## Purpose

Complements [`departments-and-programs.md`](departments-and-programs.md) and [`research-and-projects.md`](../student-life/research-and-projects.md) with concrete, named research entities. This gives the RAG system realistic entities to retrieve when a student asks "which lab works on X" instead of only general disciplinary descriptions.

## Named Research Centres

### Centre for AI and Data Systems

Affiliated primarily with Information Science and Engineering and Computer Science and Engineering. Focus areas include applied machine learning, retrieval-augmented generation, natural language processing, and data-systems research. Hosts the AI and Machine Learning Community's project mentorship track.

### Embedded Systems and IoT Lab

Affiliated primarily with Electronics and Communication Engineering. Focus areas include microcontroller-based systems, sensor networks, embedded AI, and Internet-of-Things prototyping. Frequently supplies project infrastructure for the Robotics and Embedded Systems Community.

### Cybersecurity Research Lab

Affiliated primarily with Computer Science and Engineering and Information Science and Engineering. Focus areas include applied network security, secure software development, and authorized vulnerability research. All security research and testing conducted here follows an explicit authorization and ethics process — see [`digital-conduct-and-computing.md`](../policies/digital-conduct-and-computing.md).

### Robotics and Automation Lab

Affiliated primarily with Mechanical Engineering and Electrical and Electronics Engineering. Focus areas include robotic manipulation, automation systems, and control-systems research, often combined with embedded and AI components from other departments.

### Power and Renewable Energy Lab

Affiliated primarily with Electrical and Electronics Engineering. Focus areas include power-systems analysis, renewable-energy integration, and smart-grid research.

### Materials and Manufacturing Lab

Affiliated primarily with Mechanical Engineering. Focus areas include materials characterization, additive manufacturing, and computational manufacturing design.

## Getting Involved in Research

Students interested in research at any of these centres typically:

1. identify a centre whose focus area matches their interest;
2. review any public project listings or reach out to a faculty member affiliated with that area (see [`faculty-and-departments-directory.md`](faculty-and-departments-directory.md));
3. discuss expectations, time commitment, and whether the involvement is for credit, a paid research assistantship, or voluntary experience;
4. begin with a smaller, well-scoped task before taking on independent research responsibility.

Availability of research assistantships and specific ongoing projects changes each semester and is not part of this static corpus. Campus Copilot can describe a centre's general focus area but should not claim a specific open position exists.

## Interdisciplinary Use

Several centres deliberately overlap — for example, embedded AI work spans both the Centre for AI and Data Systems and the Embedded Systems and IoT Lab. Students pursuing interdisciplinary projects (see the interdisciplinary examples in [`departments-and-programs.md`](departments-and-programs.md)) are encouraged to reach out to faculty across more than one centre.
