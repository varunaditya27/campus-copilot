# Faculty and Department Directory

## Department Structure

Every department follows the same general staffing pattern, headed by a Head of Department (HOD) who reports to the Dean of Academic Affairs. Below the HOD, faculty are organized by rank: Professors, Associate Professors, Assistant Professors, and Teaching Assistants or Lab Instructors who support laboratory sections and tutorials. Each department additionally designates a Vice-Chair for Academic Affairs, who manages timetabling and elective allotment, and a Vice-Chair for Research, who coordinates lab access, project funding requests, and publication support.

Course coordinators are assigned per course per semester, usually an Assistant or Associate Professor, and are the correct first point of contact for questions specific to that course — grading queries, assignment extensions, or content clarification. The HOD is the point of escalation only when a course-level conversation does not resolve the issue.

## Reaching a Faculty Member

The correct channel depends on what a student needs.

For a course-specific question, contact the course instructor or coordinator, either by institutional email or during posted weekly office hours. For a department-level academic matter — registration holds, elective approval, project guide allocation — contact the department office or the HOD directly. For research supervision or project guidance, identify a faculty member whose research area matches the intended project and reach out by email requesting a brief meeting; a directory of named research centres and their faculty leads is maintained separately and can help narrow this down. For general administrative matters unrelated to a specific department — fee payment, transcript requests, hostel administration — contact the relevant central office rather than an individual faculty member.

## Office Hours

Every faculty member publishes weekly office hours at the start of each semester, posted on the department notice board and circulated by the department office. Students may drop in during posted hours without a prior appointment. Outside those hours, students should email the faculty member to request a meeting rather than assume availability; most faculty respond to academic email within two working days during term.

## Visiting and Adjunct Faculty

Select electives, particularly in emerging technology areas such as applied machine learning, blockchain systems, and advanced robotics, are taught by visiting industry practitioners or adjunct faculty appointed for a single semester. These instructors may hold limited office hours and are typically reachable primarily through institutional email rather than in-person drop-in slots. Students enrolled in such electives should confirm the instructor's preferred communication channel during the first week of class.

## Directory Updates

Faculty joins, departures, and designation changes are updated in the printed department directory each semester by the Dean of Academic Affairs office. Students who find an outdated listing should report it to their department office rather than relying on informal or unverified sources.
