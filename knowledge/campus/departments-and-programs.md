# Departments and Academic Programs

## Overview

The university offers undergraduate and postgraduate engineering programs across five departments: Information Science and Engineering, Computer Science and Engineering, Electronics and Communication Engineering, Electrical and Electronics Engineering, and Mechanical Engineering. Each department admits a fixed annual intake set by the Academic Council, runs its own laboratory infrastructure, and offers a structured mix of core courses, department electives, and open electives across eight semesters for the undergraduate program.

## Information Science and Engineering

The Information Science and Engineering program focuses on computing systems, software engineering, data management, artificial intelligence, networking, and information-centric applications. Core coursework spans programming fundamentals, data structures and algorithms, database management systems, operating systems, computer networks, and software engineering practice, followed by electives in machine learning, cloud computing, big data systems, and human-computer interaction.

Typical final-year project directions include full-stack applications, intelligent recommendation systems, data platforms, developer tooling, and applied research in collaboration with faculty. The department admits 180 students each year across two sections.

## Computer Science and Engineering

Computer Science and Engineering covers computational theory and software systems, including algorithms, data structures, operating systems, computer networks, databases, programming languages, artificial intelligence, and distributed computing. The curriculum places particular emphasis on theoretical foundations in the first two years before moving into systems-heavy electives in the third and fourth years.

Students often combine coursework with internships, competitive programming, undergraduate research, and open-source contribution. The department admits 180 students each year and maintains one of the largest final-year project cohorts on campus.

## Electronics and Communication Engineering

Electronics and Communication Engineering combines analog and digital electronics, communication systems, embedded computing, signal processing, and hardware-software integration. Laboratory work is a substantial part of every semester, covering circuit design, microcontroller programming, and communication-system simulation.

Project work commonly involves microcontrollers, sensor interfacing, wireless communication protocols, FPGA-based systems, and embedded intelligence. The department admits 120 students each year.

## Electrical and Electronics Engineering

Electrical and Electronics Engineering covers electrical machines, power systems, control systems, instrumentation, and power electronics, alongside a growing set of computational electives. Core laboratories cover machines, power systems simulation, and control systems, with dedicated equipment for each.

Project work increasingly includes automation, renewable energy integration, embedded control platforms, and smart-grid applications. The department admits 90 students each year.

## Mechanical Engineering

Mechanical Engineering focuses on engineering mechanics, materials science, manufacturing processes, thermal and fluid systems, machine design, and industrial automation. The program balances classroom instruction with extensive workshop and laboratory practice, including a mandatory manufacturing practice course in the second year.

Computational design, simulation, robotics, and data-driven manufacturing feature prominently in later semesters and final-year projects. The department admits 150 students each year and operates the largest workshop floor space on campus.

## Interdisciplinary Learning

Students are encouraged to pursue coursework and projects across departmental boundaries through the open elective system, which allows any student to take a limited number of courses outside their home department starting in the third year. Common interdisciplinary combinations include biotechnology electives paired with data analysis coursework for biomedical data projects, mechanical engineering paired with computing electives for robotics and simulation work, electronics paired with artificial intelligence electives for edge intelligence projects, and information science paired with design-oriented electives for human-centered software work.

Students interested in an interdisciplinary final-year project should discuss co-guidance arrangements with faculty from both departments early in the seventh semester, since project proposals involving two departments require sign-off from both HODs.

## Changing Departments

A student wishing to transfer between departments after first-year admission must apply through the Academic Section within the first two weeks of the third semester, subject to seat availability in the receiving department and a minimum CGPA threshold set annually by the Academic Council. Transfer requests are rare and are evaluated case by case; students considering this route should first consult their current department's HOD.
