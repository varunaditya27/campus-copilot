# Dining, Transport, and Everyday Campus Logistics

## Dining

The campus includes a central cafeteria, two smaller refreshment kiosks, and a resident dining hall attached to the hostel blocks. Dining facilities are intended to provide convenient, affordable meals during academic hours as well as full meal service for resident students.

### Central Cafeteria

The Central Cafeteria, located on the ground floor of the Student Activity Centre, seats approximately 300 students and operates on the following schedule:

| Service | Hours |
| --- | --- |
| Breakfast | 8:00 a.m. – 10:30 a.m. |
| Lunch | 12:00 p.m. – 3:00 p.m. |
| Evening refreshments | 4:00 p.m. – 6:00 p.m. |
| Dinner (weekdays only) | 7:00 p.m. – 9:00 p.m. |

The cafeteria operates on a prepaid card system: students load credit onto their ID card at the cafeteria counter or through the fee portal, and purchases are deducted automatically. A basic meal (rice or roti with two curries and a side) is priced at approximately ₹60, with a full thali priced at ₹90. Menus rotate weekly and are posted at the cafeteria entrance and on the student notice board; a vegetarian counter and a separate non-vegetarian counter operate side by side during lunch and dinner service.

### Refreshment Kiosks

Two smaller kiosks — one in the Main Academic Block near the ground-floor lobby, and one in the Innovation and Computing Centre — serve tea, coffee, snacks, and packaged food between classes. Both kiosks operate from 8:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m. on academic days and accept both cash and ID-card credit.

### Hostel Dining Hall

Resident students on the mess plan take their meals at the dining hall attached to the hostel blocks (Ashoka, Nilgiri, Kaveri, and Vindhya), which serves breakfast, lunch, evening tea, and dinner on a fixed schedule set at the start of each semester by the Hostel Administration office in consultation with an elected mess committee. Students with dietary restrictions or allergies should register them with the Hostel Administration office within the first two weeks of the semester so the mess contractor can plan accordingly.

Students should follow posted hygiene, queue discipline, payment, and waste-disposal guidelines at all dining facilities. Food-safety inspections of the cafeteria and mess kitchens are conducted quarterly by Facilities and Estate Management in coordination with an external certified inspector, and inspection reports are posted on the dining notice board.

## Campus Transport

A campus shuttle service connects the main gate with the academic blocks, the hostel blocks, and the Sports Complex, running every fifteen minutes during peak hours (7:45 a.m. – 9:15 a.m. and 4:30 p.m. – 6:00 p.m.) and every thirty minutes through the rest of the academic day. The shuttle does not run on Sundays or declared holidays, and schedules are adjusted during examination periods to add an additional evening run at 9:30 p.m. from the library to the hostel blocks during the two weeks before end-semester exams. Shuttle timing changes are announced in advance through notices posted at each stop and on the student notice board.

The university also operates a limited town shuttle on Saturdays, departing the main gate at 10:00 a.m. and 4:00 p.m. and returning at 1:00 p.m. and 7:00 p.m., primarily for students needing to run errands in town. Seats are allotted on a first-come basis at the security gate and are limited to forty per trip.

## Parking

Students commuting by two-wheeler or car must register their vehicle with campus security at the start of each academic year to receive a windshield or frame sticker permitting entry to the designated student parking areas, located behind the Sports Complex and adjacent to the Innovation and Computing Centre. Unregistered vehicles are not permitted onto campus grounds and must be parked at the visitor area near the main gate. Reserved areas near the Main Academic Block and administrative wing are restricted to faculty, staff, and visitor vehicles displaying the appropriate permit, and students should not assume these areas are available even when they appear empty.

## Getting Around Campus

The Seminar Hall is located within the Innovation and Computing Centre and is commonly used for technical workshops, guest lectures, and student conferences. The Central Library sits adjacent to the Main Academic Block, connected by a covered walkway that also passes the Student Activity Centre. The Sports Complex and the four hostel blocks are located on the eastern side of campus, roughly a ten-minute walk from the Main Academic Block, which is why the shuttle service is most heavily used during the morning and evening peak windows connecting that side of campus to the academic core. Students unfamiliar with the campus layout can pick up a printed campus map from the Student Services Desk or refer to the campus map posted at each shuttle stop and building entrance.
