# Campus Services

## Student Services Desk

The Student Services Desk, located on the ground floor of the Main Academic Block, is the general first point of contact for routine campus-service questions. Staffed from 8:30 a.m. to 6:00 p.m. on weekdays and 9:00 a.m. to 1:00 p.m. on Saturdays, the desk helps students identify the appropriate office for documentation, facilities issues, student activity approvals, and general administrative processes. Most routine queries — a lost ID card, a query about which office handles a particular form, or directions to a department office — are resolved at the desk directly; anything requiring institutional authentication or approval is routed to the relevant office with a reference number the student can use to follow up.

## IT Help Desk

The IT Help Desk, located on the first floor of the Innovation and Computing Centre, handles common computing and campus technology issues: student account access, approved software installation requests, laboratory system issues, Wi-Fi connectivity, and basic device support. The desk is open Monday through Friday, 9:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m., with a reduced Saturday shift from 10:00 a.m. to 2:00 p.m. for urgent issues only.

Students reporting a technical problem should provide, where possible: their location or the affected facility, the device or service involved, the approximate time the issue started, the exact error message if any, and any troubleshooting steps already attempted. Most account-related tickets are resolved within one business day; hardware issues in shared laboratories are logged with Facilities and Estate Management if they require physical repair, which can take three to five working days depending on parts availability. Under no circumstances does IT Services staff ask a student for their account password over phone, email, or in person; students should treat any such request as fraudulent and report it immediately to the IT Help Desk.

## Career and Placement Support

Career services, coordinated jointly by department career coordinators and the Placement Cell, assist with internship preparation, resume reviews, mock interviews, career sessions, and employer event logistics. Students should verify employer-specific eligibility criteria and application deadlines through official notices posted by the Placement Cell rather than relying on prior years' criteria, since these change from company to company and year to year.

## Student Activity Support

Students planning a club event coordinate with the Student Activity Centre for venue booking, event registration, equipment requirements, and assignment of a responsible faculty or staff coordinator. Venue bookings for rooms within the Student Activity Centre can be made up to thirty days in advance through the centre's booking counter; bookings for the Seminar Hall or larger academic-block venues require additional sign-off from the Dean of Student Affairs, as described under campus governance. Event proposals expected to draw external attendees or vendors must be submitted at least fifteen working days ahead of the event date.

## Identity and Access Services

A student's institutional ID card is required for library access, hostel entry, laboratory access outside regular class hours, and identification during examinations. A lost or damaged ID card should be reported immediately to the Student Services Desk so that access can be suspended on the old card; a replacement is typically issued within two working days for a fee of ₹150, following the same replacement process used for library cards. Students are advised to keep their ID card on their person at all times on campus, since several facilities — including the library's extended-hours reading room and after-hours laboratory access — require it for entry.

## Administrative Requests

Certain requests involve personal or institutional records that cannot be handled at a general service counter and require the relevant record-holding office directly. These include:

- individual academic status and transcripts, handled by the Registrar's Office;
- fee balances and payment history, handled by Finance and Accounts;
- official attendance records, handled by the respective Department Office;
- disciplinary records, handled by the Dean of Student Affairs;
- examination results and revaluation requests, handled by the Examinations Cell;
- personal documents such as bonafide or migration certificates, handled by the Registrar's Office.

Students with a question that touches one of these categories are directed to the responsible office by the Student Services Desk rather than being given an informal estimate or answer.

## Research Centres and Facilities Support

Students and faculty working with the university's research centres — including the Centre for AI and Data Systems, the Embedded Systems and IoT Lab, the Cybersecurity Research Lab, the Robotics and Automation Lab, the Power and Renewable Energy Lab, and the Materials and Manufacturing Lab — coordinate equipment access and facility bookings through their respective centre coordinator, with overall administrative support from the Dean of Academics' office for cross-department research initiatives.

## Reporting a Problem

Students reporting a facilities issue such as a broken fixture, electrical fault, or plumbing problem should contact Facilities and Estate Management directly through the maintenance request line posted in every academic building, or route the request through the Student Services Desk if the responsible office is unclear. Urgent safety issues — electrical hazards, structural damage, or anything posing an immediate risk — should be reported to campus security first, and then logged with Facilities and Estate Management for follow-up repair.
