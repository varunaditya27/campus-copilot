# Campus Facilities

## Campus Layout

The campus is organized into several functional zones connected by covered walkways and internal roads served by the campus shuttle. The academic core sits at the centre of campus, with student residential and recreational facilities to the east.

### Main Academic Block

The Main Academic Block is the largest building on campus and houses lecture halls, faculty offices, departmental classrooms, tutorial rooms, the Registrar's Office counter, and Finance and Accounts. It is the primary venue for regular theory classes across all five departments and includes four large lecture theatres, each seating 120 to 180 students, alongside smaller classrooms used for tutorials and department electives.

### Innovation and Computing Centre

The Innovation and Computing Centre houses the university's computer laboratories, project workspaces, student technical club rooms, and the Seminar Hall. Technical workshops, coding events, and hackathons organized by student clubs are commonly held in this building, and its ground floor also hosts the IT Help Desk.

### Central Library

The Central Library is located adjacent to the Main Academic Block and provides individual study space, collaborative discussion rooms, and the university's periodicals and reference collection. Full detail on library hours, borrowing rules, and facilities is maintained separately by the Chief Librarian's office.

### Student Activity Centre

The Student Activity Centre houses spaces used by student clubs, cultural groups, project teams, and student-led events, along with the Central Cafeteria on its ground floor. Room availability within the centre depends on prior booking through the centre's booking counter, and larger events require sign-off from the Dean of Student Affairs.

### Sports Complex

The Sports Complex includes an indoor multipurpose court used for basketball, badminton, and volleyball, an outdoor cricket and football ground, a 25-metre swimming pool, and a fitness centre equipped with cardio and weight-training equipment. Facilities can generally be used during posted hours on a first-come basis, though the indoor court and pool require booking in advance through the Sports Complex office for organized practice sessions. Students should follow the safety and supervision rules applicable to each facility, particularly the pool, where a certified lifeguard must be on duty for the facility to remain open.

### Hostel Blocks

Four hostel blocks — Ashoka, Nilgiri, Kaveri, and Vindhya — house resident students on the eastern side of campus. Detailed hostel policies, room allocation, and visiting rules are maintained separately by the Hostel Administration office.

## Seminar Hall

The university's Seminar Hall, located within the Innovation and Computing Centre, seats 150 and is fitted with a projection system, a public-address system, and tiered seating. It is used for guest lectures, department workshops, technical events, project demonstrations, and student conferences. Bookings are made through the Student Activity Centre's booking counter or, for academic events, directly through the Dean of Academics' office, and should be requested at least five working days in advance for a routine booking or fifteen working days for an event expecting outside guests.

## Computer Laboratories

The university operates two general-purpose computer laboratories, Computing Lab 1 and Computing Lab 2, in the Innovation and Computing Centre, supporting coursework, programming practice, workshops, and examinations for all departments. In addition, several department-specific laboratories support specialized coursework and research: the Cybersecurity Lab, the Innovation Lab, and the Embedded Systems and IoT Lab. Students using shared systems are expected to save their work to their allotted network storage rather than the local machine, log out at the end of each session, and report any hardware fault to the IT Help Desk rather than attempting a repair themselves. Lab access outside scheduled class hours requires swiping a valid student ID at the entrance turnstile and is generally available from 8:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m. on academic days.

## Accessibility

Academic and student-service buildings on campus are fitted with ramp access at all main entrances, and the Main Academic Block, Central Library, and Innovation and Computing Centre are equipped with lifts serving all floors. Reserved seating is available in lecture theatres and the Seminar Hall for students with mobility needs; students requiring this accommodation should register with the Dean of Student Affairs at the start of the semester so arrangements can be made with individual departments. A student who needs an accessibility accommodation not currently documented by campus services should raise the request with the Student Services Desk, which will coordinate with Facilities and Estate Management and the relevant department.

## General Facility Rules

Students are expected to carry their institutional ID at all times, as several buildings and after-hours facilities require it for entry. Food and drink are restricted to designated areas within academic buildings and the library, and smoking is prohibited across the entire campus, including hostel blocks. Any damage to shared facilities or equipment should be reported to Facilities and Estate Management promptly; repair or replacement costs for damage caused by student negligence may be charged to the responsible student or student group.
