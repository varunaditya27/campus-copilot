# Health and Medical Centre

> **Demo corpus document:** This document provides general educational and logistical information for the workshop. It is not medical advice, and timings/services described are fictionalized.

## Overview

The Campus Health and Medical Centre provides first-level medical care, basic emergency response, and referral to external healthcare providers for students, faculty, and staff. It is located near the Student Activity Centre for the demonstration campus layout described in [`facilities.md`](facilities.md).

## Services

- Outpatient consultation for common illnesses and minor injuries.
- Basic first aid and emergency stabilization pending transfer to a hospital, where needed.
- A small in-house pharmacy for common over-the-counter medication.
- Referral letters for specialist care at partner hospitals.
- Health records for students who consent to maintain them on campus.

## Demonstration Operating Hours

- Monday to Saturday: 8:00 AM – 8:00 PM, outpatient consultation.
- Emergency on-call coverage: available at all times through the campus emergency contact number.
- Sunday: emergency coverage only.

As with other timing data in this corpus, these hours are illustrative. A production assistant should not assume the centre is currently open without checking a live or structured source when the timing genuinely matters (for example, an urgent situation).

## Emergency Procedure

For a medical emergency on campus:

1. Contact the campus emergency number or the nearest security post immediately.
2. If safe to do so, escort or accompany the affected person to the Health Centre or wait with them for medical staff.
3. Notify the hostel warden (for residents) or the relevant department office (during class hours) as soon as practical.
4. Follow instructions from medical staff regarding hospital transfer if required.

Campus Copilot should never attempt to diagnose a medical condition, recommend a specific medication or dosage, or delay a student from seeking real emergency help by trying to reason through symptoms conversationally. If a student describes a medical emergency, the assistant's first responsibility is to point them to real emergency channels, not to be helpful in a general knowledge-retrieval sense.

## Mental Health and Counseling Referral

The Health Centre coordinates with campus counseling support for students experiencing stress, anxiety, or other mental-health concerns; see [`student-wellbeing.md`](../student-life/student-wellbeing.md) for how Campus Copilot should handle these conversations generally. The Health Centre itself can provide an initial referral, but ongoing counseling is typically handled by dedicated counseling staff, not the medical outpatient service.

## Health Insurance

Students are typically covered by a group health insurance policy arranged through the institution for the duration of their enrollment, covering hospitalization up to a policy-defined limit. Claim procedures, network hospitals, and coverage limits are insurer-specific and change periodically; this corpus intentionally does not state specific coverage amounts, since an incorrect claim-related answer could have real financial consequences for a student in a genuine emergency.

## Records and Privacy

Health records are sensitive personal data. Campus Copilot should never ask a student to share medical history, diagnosis details, or insurance identifiers in an ordinary chat conversation, and should not claim to have access to a student's health record.
