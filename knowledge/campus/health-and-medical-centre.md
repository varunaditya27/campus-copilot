# Health and Medical Centre

## Overview

The Campus Health and Medical Centre provides first-level medical care, basic emergency response, and referral to external healthcare providers for students, faculty, and staff. The centre is located adjacent to the Student Activity Centre and is staffed by a resident Medical Officer, two staff nurses, and a rotating on-call physician for after-hours coverage.

## Services

The centre offers outpatient consultation for common illnesses and minor injuries, basic first aid and emergency stabilization pending transfer to a hospital where needed, and a small in-house pharmacy stocking common over-the-counter medication and a limited range of prescription essentials. Staff issue referral letters for specialist care at the university's network of partner hospitals, and maintain health records for students who consent to have them kept on campus. The centre also runs periodic health camps each semester covering general wellness checks, vision screening, and blood donation drives organized with a partner blood bank.

## Operating Hours

Outpatient consultation runs Monday to Saturday from 8:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m. Emergency on-call coverage is available at all times through the campus emergency number. On Sundays and public holidays, the centre provides emergency coverage only, with the on-call physician reachable through the same emergency number.

## Emergency Procedure

In a medical emergency on campus, the first step is to contact the campus emergency number or the nearest security post immediately. If it is safe to do so, the person nearby should escort or accompany the affected individual to the Health Centre, or remain with them until medical staff arrive. The hostel warden should be notified for a resident student, or the relevant department office during class hours, as soon as practical after the immediate emergency is handled. Hospital transfer, when required, is arranged by Health Centre staff in coordination with the partner hospital network, and a staff member typically accompanies the student during transfer where possible.

## Mental Health and Counseling

The Health Centre coordinates with the campus Counseling and Wellness Office for students experiencing stress, anxiety, exam-related pressure, or other mental health concerns. The Health Centre can provide an initial assessment and referral, but ongoing counseling support is handled by dedicated counseling staff rather than the medical outpatient service. Students can self-refer to the Counseling and Wellness Office directly, without going through the Health Centre first, and all counseling appointments are kept confidential except where safety requires disclosure under the university's duty-of-care policy.

## Health Insurance

All enrolled students are covered by a group health insurance policy arranged through the institution for the duration of their enrollment, covering hospitalization at network hospitals up to the policy's annual limit. The current policy covers hospitalization up to ₹2,00,000 per student per academic year, with pre-existing condition coverage subject to the insurer's standard waiting period. Claim forms are available at the Health Centre front desk and through the Accounts and Finance Office; a claim requires the discharge summary and original hospital bills to be submitted within 30 days of discharge. Students are encouraged to confirm current network hospitals and coverage details with the Health Centre each year, since insurer terms are renewed annually and can change.

## Records and Privacy

Health records held by the centre are treated as sensitive personal data and are shared only with the student's written consent, except where disclosure is required for emergency treatment or under applicable law. Students who wish to access, correct, or withdraw consent for their health record on file can do so by submitting a written request to the Medical Officer.
