# Hostel and Accommodation

> **Demo corpus document:** Block names, timings, and rules below are fictionalized workshop data.

## Overview

The demonstration campus provides on-campus hostel accommodation for students who do not commute from home. Accommodation is organized into separate blocks, each administered by a warden and supported by a resident student council.

## Hostel Blocks

- **Ashoka Block** — first-year residents, shared rooms of two to three students.
- **Nilgiri Block** — second- and third-year residents, primarily double-occupancy rooms.
- **Kaveri Block** — final-year and postgraduate residents, primarily single or double-occupancy rooms with additional study space.
- **Vindhya Block** — reserved for women residents across all years, with dedicated wardens and security arrangements.

Room allocation is handled by the Hostel Administration office (see [`administration-and-governance.md`](../administration/administration-and-governance.md)) based on year of study, availability, and any accessibility accommodations requested at admission. Campus Copilot should not claim to know a specific student's room assignment.

## Warden System

Each block has a resident or visiting warden responsible for day-to-day administration, discipline, and emergency coordination, supported by a student hostel council that helps coordinate common concerns, mess feedback, and block-level events.

## Mess and Dining

Hostel residents typically opt into a mess plan covering breakfast, lunch, and dinner, distinct from the general campus cafeteria described in [`dining-and-transport.md`](dining-and-transport.md). Mess menus rotate weekly and dietary-preference sections (vegetarian, non-vegetarian, and special dietary needs) are typically available. Specific weekly menus are operational data, not corpus content, and should come from a structured source in a production deployment.

## Timings and Access

For the demonstration dataset:

- Hostel gates: open 6:00 AM – 10:30 PM for regular movement.
- Late entry beyond 10:30 PM requires prior notice to the warden except in a documented emergency.
- Overnight outings require a filed leave request through the hostel administration process, generally with parental or guardian acknowledgment for students below a defined age or year threshold.

These timings are illustrative and should be verified against the current hostel handbook in a real deployment, since safety-related access rules should never be treated as static once real students are involved.

## Guest Policy

Visitors are generally permitted in designated common areas during daytime hours, subject to sign-in procedures at the block entrance. Guests are not permitted in resident rooms without the resident's and, where applicable, the roommate's consent, and overnight guests require prior warden approval.

## Maintenance and Complaints

Maintenance issues (electrical, plumbing, furniture, internet connectivity) should be reported to the block's maintenance desk or logged through the campus facilities request process. Urgent safety issues (electrical hazards, broken locks, water leakage near electrical points) should be reported immediately rather than through routine request channels.

## Discipline

Hostel residents are expected to follow the same code of conduct that applies campus-wide (see [`code-of-conduct-and-discipline.md`](../policies/code-of-conduct-and-discipline.md)), with additional hostel-specific rules around noise, guest access, and shared-space etiquette enforced by the warden and student council.
