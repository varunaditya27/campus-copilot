# Hostel and Accommodation

## Overview

The university provides on-campus hostel accommodation for students who do not commute from home, housing approximately 1,400 residents across four blocks. Each block is administered by a Chief Warden supported by one or more Assistant Wardens and a resident student council elected each academic year. The Hostel Administration office, located on the ground floor of the Student Activity Centre, oversees allocation, fee collection, and policy across all four blocks.

## Hostel Blocks

Ashoka Block houses first-year residents in shared rooms of two to three students across four floors, with a total capacity of 420 students. Each floor has a common washroom bay, a small pantry, and a floor representative who liaises with the Assistant Warden. The Ashoka Chief Warden's office is on the ground floor near the main entrance, and the warden holds a daily grievance window from 6:00 p.m. to 7:00 p.m.

Nilgiri Block houses second- and third-year residents, primarily in double-occupancy rooms, across five floors with a capacity of 460 students. Each floor houses roughly 90 residents and elects a floor representative each August. The Chief Warden's office is on the ground floor, with a grievance window from 7:00 p.m. to 8:00 p.m. on weekdays.

Kaveri Block houses final-year undergraduates and postgraduate residents, primarily in single or double-occupancy rooms with attached study space, across four floors with a capacity of 260 students. Rooms are somewhat larger than in the other blocks to accommodate project and thesis work. The Chief Warden's office is on the ground floor, with a grievance window from 6:30 p.m. to 7:30 p.m.

Vindhya Block is reserved for women residents across all years and academic levels, with a capacity of 380 students across five floors, and maintains its own dedicated security desk staffed around the clock in addition to the standard warden structure. The Chief Warden's office is on the ground floor near the security desk, with a grievance window from 6:00 p.m. to 7:30 p.m. daily.

## Room Allocation

Room allocation is handled by the Hostel Administration office based on year of study, seat availability, and any accessibility accommodations requested at the time of admission. Incoming first-year students are allotted a room and roommate before the start of the academic year and receive their allocation letter along with the admission offer. Returning students submit a room preference form each June for the following academic year; preferences are honored where possible but are not guaranteed, and allocation is finalized by the second week of July.

Room changes after allocation are permitted only through a formal request to the block's Chief Warden, generally considered only in cases of a documented interpersonal conflict, a health-related need, or a vacancy arising from another resident's withdrawal. Informal room swaps between residents are not permitted without the warden's written approval, since the hostel fee and mess billing are tied to a specific room and occupant record.

## Hostel Fees

Hostel fees are billed per semester and cover room rent, basic furnishing, electricity up to a per-room allowance, and Wi-Fi. For the current academic year, semester hostel fees are approximately ₹65,000 for a shared room in Ashoka or Nilgiri Block, ₹78,000 for a single or double room in Kaveri Block, and ₹68,000 for Vindhya Block, which includes the additional security staffing cost. A refundable caution deposit of ₹10,000 is collected at first allotment and adjusted against any end-of-tenancy damages. Fees are payable through the same online fee portal used for tuition, with a due date typically two weeks before the start of each semester; a late fee of ₹500 per week applies after the due date.

## Mess and Dining

Hostel residents opt into a mess plan covering breakfast, lunch, evening snacks, and dinner, distinct from the general campus cafeteria. The current mess fee is approximately ₹28,000 per semester, billed alongside hostel fees. Mess menus rotate on a two-week cycle and are posted on the notice board outside each block's dining hall, with separate vegetarian and non-vegetarian counters at lunch and dinner, and a dedicated request process for students with documented medical dietary needs, coordinated through the Health and Medical Centre.

Standard mess timings are breakfast from 7:00 a.m. to 9:00 a.m., lunch from 12:00 p.m. to 2:00 p.m., evening snacks from 5:00 p.m. to 6:00 p.m., and dinner from 7:30 p.m. to 9:30 p.m. Residents who will miss a stretch of meals due to travel can file a mess-leave request at least 24 hours in advance for a partial fee credit applied the following semester.

## Warden System

Each block's Chief Warden is a full-time, typically resident, staff member responsible for day-to-day administration, discipline, and emergency coordination, and is supported by one or two Assistant Wardens who handle routine matters and cover the Chief Warden's absence. The elected student hostel council for each block coordinates mess feedback, common-area maintenance requests, and block-level cultural events, and meets with the Chief Warden monthly. Contact for any warden is through the block's ground-floor warden office during posted grievance hours, or through the campus emergency number for urgent matters outside those hours.

## Timings and Access

Hostel gates are open from 6:00 a.m. to 10:30 p.m. for regular movement. Residents returning after 10:30 p.m. require prior notice to the Assistant Warden on duty, logged in the late-entry register, except in a documented emergency. Repeated unregistered late entry is treated as a disciplinary matter and referred to the Chief Warden.

Overnight outings require a leave request filed through the hostel administration portal at least 24 hours in advance, and first-year residents additionally require parental or guardian acknowledgment on file for any overnight leave. Leave requests for outings beyond 48 hours require the Chief Warden's approval in addition to the standard filing.

## Guest Policy

Visitors are permitted in each block's designated common area — typically the ground-floor lobby or common room — during daytime hours from 9:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m., subject to sign-in at the block entrance with a valid photo identification left at the security desk. Guests are not permitted in resident rooms without the resident's consent and, where a room is shared, the roommate's consent as well. Overnight guests require prior written approval from the Chief Warden and are limited to immediate family in most cases.

## Maintenance and Complaints

Routine maintenance issues — electrical faults, plumbing, furniture repair, or internet connectivity — should be reported to the block's maintenance desk or logged through the campus facilities request system, with a standard response window of 48 hours for non-urgent issues. Urgent safety issues, such as exposed wiring, a broken door lock, or water leakage near an electrical fixture, should be reported immediately to the warden on duty or the campus emergency number rather than through the routine request queue.

## Discipline

Hostel residents are expected to follow the campus-wide Student Code of Conduct, with additional hostel-specific rules covering quiet hours after 10:00 p.m., guest access, and shared-space etiquette. A first violation of hostel-specific rules typically results in a warning recorded by the Chief Warden; repeated or serious violations are referred to the Anti-Ragging Committee or the Disciplinary Committee as appropriate, and can result in room reassignment or, in serious cases, loss of hostel accommodation for the remainder of the academic year.
