# Administration and Governance

## Institutional Structure

The university's administration is organized into four principal streams, all reporting to the Office of the Director: Academics, Student Affairs, the Registrar's Office, and Administrative Services. Each stream is headed by a senior officer who sits on the University Management Council, which meets on the first Monday of every month to review institutional matters and approve policy changes.

```text
Office of the Director
        │
        ├── Dean of Academics
        │        └── Heads of Department (one per department)
        │                 └── Course Coordinators
```

## Office of the Director

The Director holds overall institutional responsibility and chairs the University Management Council. Matters that cannot be resolved at the department, dean, or registrar level are escalated to this office. The Director's office also approves the academic calendar each year, ratifies new student clubs, and signs off on any policy that affects fee structure or examination regulations. Students or parents seeking an audience with the Director's office should first route their request through the Student Services Desk, which schedules appointments on the second and fourth Wednesday of each month.

## Dean of Academics

The Dean of Academics oversees curriculum design, cross-department academic policy, and coordination of the academic calendar across all five departments. The office also chairs the Academic Council, which approves new electives, reviews grading appeals that have been escalated beyond the department level, and ratifies changes to credit requirements. Heads of Department report to the Dean of Academics on matters of curriculum delivery, faculty allocation for courses, and academic scheduling.

## Heads of Department

Each of the five engineering departments — Information Science and Engineering, Computer Science and Engineering, Electronics and Communication Engineering, Electrical and Electronics Engineering, and Mechanical Engineering — is led by a Head of Department (HOD). The HOD is responsible for faculty allocation within the department, oversight of curriculum delivery, department-level student concerns including academic counselling, and the department's research and laboratory direction. Each department also appoints Course Coordinators for individual subjects, who handle day-to-day queries about a specific course, assignment deadlines, and internal assessment schedules. A student with a concern that a course instructor cannot resolve should first approach the Course Coordinator, then the HOD if the matter remains unresolved.

## Dean of Student Affairs

The Dean of Student Affairs is the primary point of escalation for non-academic student matters. This office oversees the Student Activity Centre and the approval of campus-wide events, the Hostel Administration office, and the university's Discipline and Grievance Committees. Any club seeking to host an event expected to draw more than 200 attendees, or requiring outside vendors on campus, must route its proposal through this office at least fifteen working days in advance. The Dean of Student Affairs also chairs the Anti-Ragging Committee and the Internal Complaints Committee, both of which meet monthly or on an emergency basis if a complaint is filed.

## Registrar's Office

The Registrar's Office administers admissions, the Examinations Cell, and the official academic record system, including transcripts, degree verification letters, migration certificates, and bonafide certificates. Official documents are issued only through the Registrar's Office counter in the Main Academic Block, open Monday through Friday from 9:30 a.m. to 4:30 p.m., and Saturday from 9:30 a.m. to 12:30 p.m. A standard transcript request takes five working days and costs ₹200 per copy, with an expedited two-day turnaround available for ₹400; a bonafide certificate is issued within two working days, free for the first two requests each academic year and ₹100 per copy thereafter. Degree verification requests routed to an external employer or foreign university typically take seven to ten working days.

## Administrative Services

Finance and Accounts handles fee collection, refund processing, and scholarship disbursement, and maintains a help counter in the Main Academic Block during business hours. IT Services maintains the campus network, student and staff computing accounts, and laboratory infrastructure across all departments; day-to-day technical issues are handled by the IT Help Desk. Facilities and Estate Management is responsible for the upkeep of buildings, utilities, groundskeeping, and campus infrastructure, and maintains a maintenance request line for reporting issues such as electrical faults, plumbing, or classroom equipment failures.

## Committees and Councils

Beyond the reporting structure above, the university maintains several standing committees with student representation. The Student Grievance Redressal Committee, convened by the Dean of Student Affairs, includes two elected student representatives from each department and meets on the third Friday of every month. The Curriculum Review Board, under the Dean of Academics, reviews department syllabi once every two years and includes invited alumni and industry members. Students interested in serving as a class representative or committee member should watch for nomination notices posted at the start of each academic year by the Student Activity Centre.

## Escalation Guidance

A student unsure which office handles a particular matter should begin at the Student Services Desk, which can direct routine questions to the correct office. As a general rule: academic scheduling and curriculum questions go to the Department Office or the Dean of Academics; hostel, discipline, and event matters go to the Dean of Student Affairs; transcripts, certificates, and admissions questions go to the Registrar's Office; and fee, network, or facilities issues go to the relevant unit within Administrative Services.
