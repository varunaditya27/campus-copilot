# Admissions and Fee Structure

## Admission Routes

Undergraduate admission to the university's engineering programs is offered through four routes:

- **Merit-based entrance admission** — the majority of seats are filled based on a candidate's rank in the state engineering entrance examination, followed by a centralized counselling and seat-allotment round conducted jointly with the state admissions authority.
- **Management-quota admission** — a fixed 15% of seats in each department are set aside for management-quota admission, administered directly by the Admissions Office based on qualifying examination marks and the entrance examination score, where available.
- **Lateral entry** — diploma holders from a recognized polytechnic may be admitted directly into the third semester of an appropriate engineering program, subject to availability of lateral-entry seats (typically 10% of the sanctioned intake per department) and a qualifying lateral-entry entrance test conducted in June each year.
- **Transfer admission** — a student from another recognized engineering program may apply for transfer admission into the second or third semester, subject to seat availability, a satisfactory academic record, and approval by the Dean of Academics. Transfer admissions are rare and typically limited to fewer than five students per department per year.

Admission cutoffs and seat matrices are published fresh each academic year by the Admissions Office ahead of the counselling round, along with the department-wise sanctioned intake for that year.

## Sanctioned Intake

Undergraduate intake varies by department based on demand and laboratory capacity: 180 each for Information Science and Engineering and Computer Science and Engineering, 120 for Electronics and Communication Engineering, 90 for Electrical and Electronics Engineering, and 150 for Mechanical Engineering, giving a total annual undergraduate intake of 720 students across the five departments. Postgraduate programs admit smaller cohorts, typically 24 to 36 students per department per year, filled primarily through a national postgraduate entrance score.

## Annual Fee Structure

Fees are charged per academic year and are payable in two equal instalments, one at the start of each semester. The illustrative fee structure for the current academic year for an undergraduate engineering program is as follows:

| Component | Annual Amount (₹) | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tuition Fee | 1,25,000 | Core academic instruction |
| Development Fee | 18,000 | Infrastructure and facility upkeep |
| Examination Fee | 6,000 | Covers both semester examinations |
| Laboratory Fee | 12,000 | Varies slightly by department; Mechanical and ECE carry a ₹2,000 supplement for consumables |
| Student Activity Fee | 2,500 | Clubs, cultural events, and the Student Activity Centre |
| Hostel Fee | 65,000–78,000 | Resident students only; varies by block (see the hostel fee schedule) |
| Mess Fee | 28,000 | Students opting into the dining plan; billed separately from the hostel fee |
| Caution Deposit | 10,000 | One-time, refundable at course completion less any dues |

A day-scholar undergraduate student's recurring annual charge (tuition, development, examination, laboratory, and student activity fees) comes to approximately ₹1,63,500; including the one-time caution deposit, the first year totals approximately ₹1,73,500, dropping back to ₹1,63,500 in subsequent years. A resident student in a shared room (Ashoka or Nilgiri Block) opting into the mess plan pays approximately ₹2,66,500 in the first year, rising to roughly ₹2,79,500 for a Kaveri Block single or double room. Postgraduate tuition is charged at ₹95,000 per year; the remaining components are the same as the undergraduate schedule.

Lateral-entry students are charged the standard fee for their entry semester onward and are not charged first- and second-semester fees they did not attend. Management-quota admission carries a one-time, non-refundable admission fee of ₹75,000 in addition to the standard annual fee schedule above.

## Payment Windows

Semester fees are due before the start of each semester's teaching period: on or before July 15 for the odd semester and December 15 for the even semester, per the academic calendar published by the Registrar's Office. A late-payment window of ten calendar days follows each due date, during which a late fee of ₹500 per week or part thereof applies. Fees unpaid beyond the late-payment window may result in the student being barred from attending classes and appearing in internal assessments until dues are cleared, per policy set by Finance and Accounts. A student anticipating difficulty meeting a payment deadline should contact Finance and Accounts before the due date; extensions granted in advance are far easier to arrange than after a deadline has passed.

Payments can be made online through the fee portal linked from the student dashboard, by demand draft payable to the university at the Finance and Accounts counter in the Main Academic Block, or by NEFT transfer using the account details published each semester along with the fee due notice.

## Fee-Related Support

Students facing financial difficulty may apply for a structured payment plan through Finance and Accounts, which can split a semester's fee into up to three monthly instalments on a case-by-case basis. Students should also consult the university's scholarship and financial-support programs, which include merit scholarships, need-based fee waivers, and department-sponsored assistantships for postgraduate students. Applications for need-based support are reviewed by the Scholarship Committee, which meets at the start of each semester.

## Refunds and Withdrawal

A student withdrawing from the program before the start of classes is eligible for a refund of the tuition and development fee components, less a ₹5,000 processing charge, provided the request is submitted in writing to the Registrar's Office. Withdrawal within the first two weeks of a semester carries a 50% refund of tuition for that semester; withdrawal after the fourth week of a semester carries no tuition refund. The caution deposit is refunded in full, less any outstanding dues or damage charges, within 45 days of the student's formal exit clearance being completed by the Registrar's Office. Hostel and mess fees follow a separate pro-rated refund schedule administered by the Hostel Administration office, based on the number of complete weeks of residence.

## Documentation Required at Admission

Candidates confirming an offer of admission must submit original mark sheets and certificates for the qualifying examination, a transfer certificate from the previous institution, a migration certificate where applicable, four passport-size photographs, and proof of category or reservation status if claiming a reserved-category seat. Original documents are verified and returned the same day; photocopies are retained by the Admissions Office for the student file. Failure to complete document verification within seven days of provisional admission may result in forfeiture of the seat.
