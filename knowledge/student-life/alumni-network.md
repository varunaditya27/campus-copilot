# Alumni Network and Mentorship

> **Demo corpus document:** Program names and descriptions below are fictionalized workshop data.

## Purpose

The alumni network connects current students with graduates for mentorship, career guidance, and community continuity. This document is scoped to the relationship structure, not a real alumni directory.

## Alumni Association

The Alumni Association maintains contact with graduates, organizes the annual Alumni Meet (see [`fests-and-cultural-events.md`](fests-and-cultural-events.md)), and coordinates giving-back programs such as guest lectures, mentorship, and scholarship sponsorship.

## Mentorship Program

Current students can opt into a structured mentorship program pairing them with an alumnus or alumna working in a related field. A typical mentorship relationship involves:

```text
Sign-up / interest form
      ↓
Matching (by department, interest area, or career goal)
      ↓
Introductory conversation
      ↓
Periodic check-ins over a semester or academic year
      ↓
Optional extension or transition to informal networking
```

Matching is handled by the Alumni Association based on submitted interest forms, not something Campus Copilot can perform directly — the assistant can explain the program and help a student think through what they want from a mentor, but cannot enroll them in the program itself in this demonstration application.

## Guest Lectures and Industry Talks

Alumni are frequently invited to give guest lectures, participate in panel discussions during the technical fest, or contribute to career-development sessions organized by the Placement Cell (see [`placement-cell.md`](../administration/placement-cell.md)).

## Giving Back

Alumni contribute to the institution in several ways beyond mentorship: sponsoring scholarships (see [`scholarships-and-financial-support.md`](scholarships-and-financial-support.md)), funding lab equipment or research centres (see [`labs-and-research-centres.md`](../campus/labs-and-research-centres.md)), and recruiting through the Placement Cell.

## Staying Connected After Graduation

Graduating students are typically invited to join the Alumni Association's own communication channels and directory. Campus Copilot, as a current-student-facing assistant, does not manage alumni-side accounts or communications — that relationship transitions to the Alumni Association's own systems after graduation.
