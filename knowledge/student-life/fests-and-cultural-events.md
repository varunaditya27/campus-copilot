# Fests and Campus-Wide Events

## Purpose

Beyond regular club activities and academic-calendar milestones, the university's student life is punctuated each year by a set of large, campus-wide events — technical and cultural fests, an annual sports tournament, orientation for incoming students, and department-level celebrations. This document describes what each of these events typically involves and roughly when it falls in the academic year, so students and families can plan around them.

## Annual Technical Fest

The university's flagship technical festival is a three-day, campus-wide event typically held in the even semester, generally in the last week of February or first week of March. It combines competitive events — a 24-hour flagship hackathon, coding contests, a robotics challenge, and a capture-the-flag security competition — with technical workshops, guest talks from industry practitioners and academic researchers, and a project exhibition where student teams from all five departments demonstrate work built over the preceding semester. The four technical communities each run signature events under the fest umbrella: the Coding Club typically anchors the coding contests, the AI and Machine Learning Community runs a themed hackathon track, the Cybersecurity Community runs the capture-the-flag competition, and the Robotics and Embedded Systems Community runs the robotics challenge.

The fest is organized by a student fest committee of around 40 volunteers working under the Dean of Student Affairs' office, with planning beginning roughly four months in advance. Attendance has grown steadily since the fest's founding, drawing participants from over 30 partner colleges in recent years alongside the home student body.

## Annual Cultural Fest

The cultural fest is a three-day event typically held in the odd semester, generally in late September or early October, featuring music, dance, drama, literary events, fine arts, and inter-college cultural competitions. A separate student cultural committee, elected each academic year, coordinates logistics, sponsorship outreach, and the event schedule, working alongside student music, dance, and drama circles based in the Student Activity Centre. The fest typically closes with a headline evening concert on its final night, held on the open grounds adjoining the Sports Complex.

## Sports Fest and Intramurals

An annual inter-department sports tournament spans cricket, football, basketball, badminton, athletics, table tennis, and chess, and is typically held toward the end of the academic year, generally in April. Each department fields teams across the contested sports, with points accumulated across events determining an overall departmal championship trophy awarded at a closing ceremony. Alongside the annual tournament, smaller intramural leagues run continuously throughout the year in several sports, open to any student regardless of whether they are trying out for or already part of an institutional team, and coordinated through the Sports Office.

## Orientation Week

At the start of each academic year, incoming students go through a five-day orientation covering campus familiarization, an explanation of the academic system and grading structure, introductions to all recognized clubs and communities, hostel settling-in for resident students, and administrative onboarding such as ID card issuance and library registration. Orientation Week is coordinated jointly by the Dean of Student Affairs' office and the Office of the Registrar, with department-specific sessions led by each department's faculty coordinators. Senior student volunteers, organized as orientation mentors, are assigned in small groups to new students to help them settle in during the first two weeks of the semester.

## Alumni Meet

An annual gathering held each December connects current students with graduates of the university, typically combined with mentorship program sign-ups, industry networking sessions, and panel discussions featuring alumni now working across product companies, service companies, core-engineering firms, and startups. The event is organized by the Office of Alumni Relations, which maintains the university's broader alumni engagement programs throughout the year independent of this single event.

## Department Day Celebrations

Each of the five departments holds its own annual department day, distinct from the campus-wide technical fest, typically combining technical talks from invited alumni and industry speakers, department-specific competitions, and informal faculty-student interaction sessions. These are generally smaller, single-day events organized by each department's student committee in coordination with its faculty coordinator, and dates vary department to department across the academic year.
