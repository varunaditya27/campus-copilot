# Sports and Recreation

> **Demo corpus document:** Facility details, team names, and schedules below are fictionalized workshop data.

## Overview

The Sports Complex, introduced briefly in [`facilities.md`](../campus/facilities.md), is the hub for both competitive and recreational athletics on the demonstration campus.

## Facilities

- An outdoor multi-purpose ground for football, cricket, and athletics practice.
- Indoor courts for badminton, basketball, and table tennis.
- A fitness centre with basic strength and cardio equipment.
- A swimming pool, seasonally maintained.

Facility booking for team practice follows a scheduling process coordinated through the Sports Office; casual/recreational use during open hours generally does not require booking, subject to capacity.

## Institutional Teams

The demonstration campus fields inter-collegiate teams in cricket, football, basketball, badminton, athletics, and chess, trying out students at the start of each academic year. Selection is based on trials conducted by the Sports Office and coaching staff, not something Campus Copilot has visibility into.

## Intramural Leagues

Alongside institutional teams, department-level intramural leagues run through the academic year in several sports, culminating in the annual Sports Fest described in [`fests-and-cultural-events.md`](fests-and-cultural-events.md). Intramurals are open to any interested student regardless of institutional-team status.

## Recreational Use

Outside of practice and tournament bookings, the Sports Complex is generally open for casual student use during posted hours. Students should follow facility-specific safety rules (for example, swimming pool lifeguard hours, gym equipment orientation requirements) rather than assuming unrestricted access.

## Balancing Sports and Academics

Campus Copilot can help a student build a schedule that accounts for regular practice commitments alongside academic workload, using the same planning approach described in [`student-wellbeing.md`](student-wellbeing.md). Students on institutional teams with travel commitments for tournaments should coordinate attendance accommodations with their course instructors in advance — see [`attendance-and-academic-standing.md`](../academics/attendance-and-academic-standing.md) for how attendance shortfalls are generally handled, since Campus Copilot cannot guarantee that a specific sports commitment will be treated as an attendance exception.
