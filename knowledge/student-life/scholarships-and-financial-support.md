# Scholarships and Financial Support

## Overview

The university offers financial support to students through several channels: merit-based scholarships, need-based assistance, academic and competition awards, externally funded scholarships, fee-support programs, and an emergency assistance fund. All financial support is administered by the Financial Aid Office, located alongside the Accounts Section in the Main Academic Block, though individual schemes may require additional approval from a department or the Dean of Student Affairs. Eligibility criteria, amounts, and deadlines are reviewed at the start of each academic year, so students should confirm current terms with the Financial Aid Office before applying rather than relying solely on the previous year's figures.

## Merit-Based Support

The University Merit Scholarship is awarded each semester to the top-ranked student in each department based on semester GPA, provided the student has no active disciplinary record and no backlog. The award covers ₹25,000 toward the following semester's tuition and is credited directly against the fee account by the Accounts Section; no separate application is required, as eligibility is determined automatically from examination results.

The Director's List Award recognizes the top three percent of students by cumulative GPA at the end of each academic year, calculated across all five departments. Recipients receive a certificate and a one-time award of ₹10,000, along with priority consideration for research assistantships and lab access during the following year.

Departmental toppers in specific subjects, competition winners representing the university at inter-collegiate technical events, and students with notable research publications may also be nominated by their department for a discretionary merit award, ranging typically from ₹5,000 to ₹15,000 depending on the achievement, subject to approval by the Dean of Student Affairs.

## Need-Based Support

The Need-Based Fee Assistance Programme is intended for students whose family circumstances make paying the full semester fee difficult. Awards are tiered based on verified family income and documented circumstances, and typically range from a 25 percent to a 75 percent reduction in that semester's tuition component, applied directly to the fee account.

Applications require a completed financial assistance form, the previous year's family income documentation, and, where applicable, supporting evidence of circumstances such as a parent's job loss, medical expenses, or other financial hardship. Applications are accepted twice a year, in the first two weeks of the semester, and are reviewed by a three-member Financial Aid Committee consisting of the Dean of Student Affairs, a Financial Aid Officer, and one faculty representative. Decisions are typically communicated within three weeks of the application deadline. All financial and medical documentation submitted is handled confidentially by the Financial Aid Office and is not shared outside the review committee.

## External Scholarships

Students may also apply for scholarships administered by government bodies, corporate foundations, professional engineering associations, and other external organizations. The Financial Aid Office maintains a running list of externally sponsored scholarships that past students have successfully used, along with each scheme's typical application window, but since these programs are run by outside bodies, their eligibility rules, portals, and deadlines are set independently and can change from year to year. Students pursuing an external scholarship should always confirm current details directly on the sponsoring organization's own application portal.

The Financial Aid Office can provide a bonafide student certificate, income-eligibility supporting letters, and academic transcripts needed for most external scholarship applications; these are typically issued within five working days of a request submitted at the Student Services Desk.

## Fee-Support and Installment Options

Students facing a temporary cash-flow difficulty rather than a long-term need can apply to pay a semester's fee in two installments instead of one lump sum, subject to a small administrative surcharge of ₹500. Installment requests are made through the Accounts Section before the standard fee deadline and are generally approved without requiring the fuller documentation needed for need-based assistance.

## Emergency Assistance Fund

The Dean of Student Affairs maintains a discretionary Emergency Assistance Fund for students facing a sudden, unforeseen financial crisis — for example a medical emergency, a family bereavement involving travel, or loss of essential personal belongings such as a laptop needed for coursework. Awards from this fund are modest, typically between ₹2,000 and ₹20,000 depending on the situation, and are intended as a bridge rather than a full solution. Students seeking emergency assistance should approach the Dean of Student Affairs directly, in person or through the Student Services Desk; requests are handled on an expedited basis, often within 48 hours.

## Application Workflow

Most scholarship and financial-support schemes follow a similar sequence:

1. Identify the relevant scheme and confirm current eligibility criteria with the Financial Aid Office.
2. Note the application deadline, which varies by scheme.
3. Gather the required supporting documents — income certificates, academic transcripts, or achievement records as applicable.
4. Complete the application form, available at the Financial Aid Office or its section of the university's Student Services Desk.
5. Submit the application before the deadline, retaining the acknowledgment receipt.
6. Track the application's status by checking with the Financial Aid Office directly, quoting the acknowledgment number.

Students who are uncertain which scheme applies to their situation are encouraged to speak with the Financial Aid Office directly rather than guess; a single short conversation is often enough to identify the two or three programs a given student is actually eligible for.
