# Scholarships and Financial Support

> **Demo corpus:** The schemes and eligibility descriptions below are fictionalized examples for teaching RAG. They are not official scholarship rules.

## Overview

Financial support can take several forms, including merit-based scholarships, need-based assistance, academic awards, external scholarships, fee-support programs, and emergency assistance. Eligibility and deadlines vary, so students should always verify current requirements with the responsible office.

## Merit-Based Support

Merit-based support recognizes academic performance or achievement in a defined period. Selection may consider semester performance, cumulative performance, competition results, research, innovation, or other documented achievements.

A student asking, "Am I eligible?" should not receive a definitive answer unless the relevant eligibility criteria and the student's verified records are available.

## Need-Based Support

Need-based assistance is intended to support students facing financial constraints. Applications may require supporting documentation. The assistant should never ask students to paste sensitive financial documents into a public chat or store them in an unprotected application.

## External Scholarships

Students may also apply for scholarships administered by government bodies, foundations, companies, professional associations, or other organizations. These programs can have different application portals and document requirements.

The knowledge base should include a source and validity date for any real external scholarship before it is used in a production system.

## Application Workflow

A generic scholarship workflow looks like:

```text
Find opportunity
      ↓
Read eligibility criteria
      ↓
Check deadline
      ↓
Collect required documents
      ↓
Complete application
      ↓
Submit
      ↓
Track status
```

Campus Copilot can help students understand a documented workflow and create a checklist. It should not claim to have submitted an application unless an explicit submission tool exists and the user has confirmed the action.
