# Examinations and Evaluation

> **Demo corpus document:** This is fictionalized workshop material, not an official examination regulation.

## Academic Evaluation Model

The demonstration campus uses a blended evaluation model. A course may combine continuous assessment with a final examination or final project. Continuous components can include quizzes, assignments, laboratory work, presentations, class participation, project reviews, and internal examinations.

The exact weight assigned to each component belongs to the course and semester configuration. Students should therefore ask for the specific course policy when they need a precise grading calculation.

## Internal Assessment

Internal assessment is intended to provide feedback during the semester rather than serving as a single high-stakes measurement. A typical course may have multiple assessment points so that students have several opportunities to demonstrate progress.

When answering questions about internal marks, Campus Copilot should avoid assuming that every course uses the same number of tests or the same weighting. If the relevant course document is not present in the corpus, the assistant should say so rather than fabricate a scheme.

## End-Semester Examination

The end-semester examination is normally conducted according to an academic timetable published before the examination period. Students should verify the final timetable, examination venue, reporting time, permitted materials, and any course-specific instructions from official announcements.

A student asking, "When is my exam?" requires a timetable or structured data source. A general examination-policy document cannot reliably answer a date-specific question.

## Laboratory Evaluation

Laboratory courses may evaluate preparation, attendance, implementation, observation, record work, viva performance, and a final practical assessment. Students should keep their laboratory records current and follow the instructor's submission requirements.

## Projects

Project-based courses may use milestone reviews. A typical workflow is:

```text
Topic selection
      ↓
Proposal / problem definition
      ↓
Initial review
      ↓
Implementation
      ↓
Progress review
      ↓
Final demonstration
      ↓
Report / documentation
```

The assistant can help students understand the process, but it should not invent deadlines or grading percentages without a source.

## Revaluation and Academic Queries

Students who believe an evaluation has been recorded incorrectly should follow the institution's official process for clarification, verification, or revaluation. Campus Copilot should explain the documented process and identify the responsible office when that information exists in the knowledge base.

## Exam-Day Checklist

Before an examination, a student should generally verify:

- examination date and time;
- examination venue;
- student identification requirements;
- permitted stationery or materials;
- reporting time;
- special instructions for laboratory or practical examinations.
