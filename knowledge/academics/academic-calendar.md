# Academic Calendar and Semester Lifecycle

> **Demo corpus document:** Dates and rules are illustrative workshop data.

## Semester Lifecycle

A typical engineering semester follows a predictable sequence even though exact dates vary from one academic year to another. The demonstration calendar uses the following lifecycle:

```text
Semester orientation
      ↓
Course registration
      ↓
Teaching period
      ↓
Continuous assessment
      ↓
Mid-semester evaluation
      ↓
Teaching completion
      ↓
End-semester examination
      ↓
Evaluation and result processing
      ↓
Break / next-semester preparation
```

## Important Academic Events

Students generally need to keep track of several categories of dates:

### Registration

Course registration confirms the student's academic selections for the semester. Changes to registered courses may have a defined window and should be made through the official academic process.

### Add / Drop or Course Changes

If an institution permits course changes, students should complete them within the published window. A late request may require approval and should never be assumed to be automatically accepted.

### Assessment Periods

Internal assessments, quizzes, laboratory evaluations, project reviews, and presentations may occur throughout the teaching period. Course instructors are the authoritative source for course-specific deadlines.

### Examination Period

The examination period follows completion of scheduled teaching. The final timetable should always be treated as the authoritative source for a student's specific examination date and venue.

### Results

After evaluation, results are processed and published through the institution's official channels. Students should use the official academic record when making decisions about progression or eligibility.

## Planning Advice

A student asking the assistant to create an academic plan should provide the semester, subjects, known deadlines, and available study time. Campus Copilot can organize information from the corpus, but it should not invent dates that are not present in the dataset.

## Date-Sensitive Questions

The agent should distinguish between general policy and live schedule data.

For example:

> "When does the semester end?"

requires calendar data.

Whereas:

> "What normally happens before end-semester examinations?"

can be answered from this document.
