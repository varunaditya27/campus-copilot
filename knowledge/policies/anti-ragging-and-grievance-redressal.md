# Anti-Ragging and Grievance Redressal

> **Demo corpus document:** This is a fictionalized educational policy for the Campus Copilot workshop. It illustrates how a campus assistant should handle safety-critical topics responsibly — it is not a substitute for a real institution's actual anti-ragging or grievance policy.

## Anti-Ragging Policy

Ragging — any act that causes physical or psychological harm, humiliation, or harassment of a student, particularly targeting new or junior students — is strictly prohibited on the demonstration campus in any form, including verbal, physical, online, or hostel-based ragging.

### Reporting Ragging

A student experiencing or witnessing ragging should report it immediately through any of the following channels:

- the campus Anti-Ragging Committee, whose contact details are published during orientation and posted in hostel blocks;
- the hostel warden, for hostel-based incidents;
- the Dean of Student Affairs office directly;
- the campus emergency contact number, if there is any immediate safety concern.

Reports can typically be made confidentially, and the institution's anti-ragging process is designed to protect the reporting student from retaliation. Campus Copilot should treat any mention of ragging as a priority-escalation topic: acknowledge the seriousness, provide the correct reporting channel, and avoid asking the student to describe traumatic details in the chat itself beyond what's needed to point them to help.

### What Campus Copilot Should Never Do

- Minimize or question whether an experience "really counts" as ragging.
- Attempt to mediate, investigate, or resolve the incident itself.
- Delay directing the student to a real reporting channel in favor of continuing a general conversation.

## Internal Complaints Committee (Sexual Harassment)

The Internal Complaints Committee (ICC) handles complaints of sexual harassment involving students, faculty, or staff, following the institution's documented process for confidential reporting, investigation, and resolution. As with ragging, Campus Copilot's role when this topic comes up is to point the student to the ICC's reporting channel promptly and respectfully, not to investigate or advise on the substance of the complaint.

## General Grievance Redressal Committee

For grievances that are not safety-critical — academic disputes, fee disagreements, hostel administrative issues, or other non-emergency concerns — the institution maintains a general Grievance Redressal Committee. A typical process:

```text
Submit grievance (in writing, through the designated channel)
      ↓
Acknowledgment within a defined window
      ↓
Review / investigation
      ↓
Resolution or escalation
      ↓
Communication of outcome to the student
```

Academic grade-specific grievances follow the path described in [`grading-and-backlogs.md`](../academics/grading-and-backlogs.md); this general committee handles broader administrative and interpersonal grievances that don't fit a more specific document.

## Priority Ordering for Campus Copilot

When a student's message could plausibly involve ragging, harassment, or an immediate safety concern, that possibility should take priority over any other interpretation of the message — including any structured request (like an event registration) mentioned in the same conversation. Route the student to real help first.
